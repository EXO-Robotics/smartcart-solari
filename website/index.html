The workflow

From recipe source to retailer handoff

Every step stays reviewable. SmartCart helps organize the work; the shopper remains in control.

1

Bring in a recipe

Use a camera capture, saved photo, pasted ingredient text, supported recipe page, or included sample. Image text recognition runs with Apple Vision on the device.

2

Review the list

Edit parsed ingredients, scale servings, mark pantry items, and apply saved organic, dietary, budget, or store-brand preferences.

3

Shop with context

Review exact seeded product records and explicit search fallbacks, save the manifest, then open each retailer destination yourself.

Product truth

Clear about what is—and is not—connected

The current build is designed to prove the planning flow without presenting demo commerce data as live.

Included in the candidate

  • Recipe import and ingredient editing
  • Serving and package calculations
  • Persistent pantry and shopping preferences
  • Offline demo UPC lookup and device barcode scanning
  • Saved manifests and guided product handoff
  • Seeded exact records plus labeled search fallbacks

Not included

  • Live catalog, inventory, or price refresh
  • Production retailer authentication or cart creation
  • Pickup reservations or substitutions
  • Payment, checkout, or order submission
  • Production telemetry, cloud sync, or hosted accounts
  • Approved retailer or delivery-provider partnerships
Demo data notice: store details, distances, pickup windows, product availability, and observed prices in this beta are prototype fixtures or seeded records. A retailer determines final product, price, availability, tax, fees, substitutions, and fulfillment.
