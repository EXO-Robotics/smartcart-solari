About the project

Shopping preparation, made inspectable.

SmartCart explores a simple idea: a grocery helper should make its interpretation visible before sending a shopper anywhere.

Current status: SmartCart is private proprietary software and an unreleased human-validation candidate. This package does not announce public availability, a company, funding, customers, retailer approval, or a launch date.

What SmartCart is testing

Recipes are written for cooking, not shopping. Quantities need scaling, pantry items need decisions, and ingredient names need to become packages a store can sell. SmartCart models that translation as a sequence the shopper can review: import, edit, apply preferences, resolve products, confirm a manifest, and open retailer destinations.

Design principles

Show the source

Product records carry source and observation context. Search fallbacks remain visibly different from exact records.

Keep the shopper in control

The beta does not create a cart or complete checkout. It prepares a guided handoff for manual review.

Persist useful decisions

Pantry choices, preferences, replacements, saved manifests, and progress can survive relaunch.

Fail honestly

Unsupported recipe pages can fail, unknown products become labeled searches, and unavailable prices stay unavailable.
