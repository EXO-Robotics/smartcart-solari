AI-directed product work

AI accelerated the build.
Judgment shaped the product.

The conversion improved when technically valid work was challenged against usefulness, trust, and the existing SmartCart contract.

  1. 01
    Rejected the demo boundary

    Moved Solari into SmartCart’s native post-pantry, pre-retailer workflow.

  2. 02
    Rejected the three-item special case

    Generalized variable trips with explicit partial coverage and safe skips.

  3. 03
    Rejected invented certainty

    Kept unsupported quantities, prices, and confidence nullable or qualitative.

  4. 04
    Rejected shopper-useless math

    Made the premium-versus-waste decision legible in real product terms.
