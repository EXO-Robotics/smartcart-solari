ORIGINAL SMARTCART / 04 STEPS

The intelligence stopped at the store.

  1. 01Understand the recipeExtract ingredients and required quantities.
  2. 02Apply pantry exclusionsRemove what the shopper already has.
  3. 03Build the shopping listAggregate the remaining trip requirements.
  4. 04Open a retailerLeave package search and comparison to the shopper.

SmartCart knew what was needed, but not which current packages sensibly satisfied the trip.

SOLARI-ENHANCED / 06 STEPS

The handoff becomes a grounded decision.

  1. 01Keep SmartCart’s intentRecipe, pantry, identity, and quantities remain authoritative.
  2. 02Request researchThe shopper intentionally starts the optional step.
  3. 03Observe product evidenceBrowser records identity, package, visible synthetic price, source, and time.
  4. 04Evaluate complete basketsSandbox compares coverage, cost, and overage under bounded policy.
  5. 05Validate in SmartCartThe app checks the evidence before presenting a native recommendation.
  6. 06Hand control backThe shopper chooses whether to continue to the retailer.

The reusable architecture

The frontend is replaceable.
Solari is the capability.

SmartCart is the first complete use case, not the limit. Any product that understands structured intent but loses intelligence at the external web can use the same integration pattern.
