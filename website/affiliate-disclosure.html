Affiliate disclosure

The current beta package does not represent affiliate compensation, retailer partnership, endorsement, or approved live integration.

Current private-beta position

The SmartCart validation candidate uses a demo retailer adapter with seeded product records, exact public product destinations where present, and clearly labeled retailer-search fallbacks. It does not use a live retailer catalog feed and does not claim a retailer-approved integration.

This website package contains no affiliate-tracking parameters, commerce scripts, advertising tags, or monetized calls to action. It makes no claim that SmartCart earns a commission when a tester opens or purchases from a retailer destination.

No implied affiliation

Retailer and delivery-provider names, if referenced, identify destinations, demo data, or experimental concepts. They do not imply sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, approval, or ownership of SmartCart.

If monetization is added later

Before adding compensated links or referrals, the operator should:

  • confirm program approval and follow the program’s trademark and disclosure rules;
  • label compensated links clearly and near the relevant recommendation;
  • explain whether compensation affects ranking or presentation;
  • update the privacy notice for tracking, cookies, identifiers, and vendors;
  • test that links and disclosures remain accessible across screen sizes;
  • retain records showing which links are compensated.
