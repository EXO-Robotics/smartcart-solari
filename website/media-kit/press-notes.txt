Status
SmartCart is a private iOS beta. This file does not announce public availability,
a company, a launch date, customers, funding, retailer approval, or partnerships.

One-sentence description
SmartCart is a private iOS beta that turns recipes into reviewable shopping
manifests, using seeded demo product records and guided retailer links without
creating a retailer cart or completing checkout.

Current private-candidate workflow
Import recipe; review ingredients; adjust servings; check pantry; apply shopping
preferences; select one prototype store; resolve seeded exact records or labeled
retailer-search fallbacks; confirm and save a manifest; open guided retailer
destinations.

Capability boundary
- No live price, inventory, store, or pickup-window feed.
- No retailer authentication, cart creation, wishlist update, or basket transfer.
- No pickup reservation, substitution submission, payment, order, or checkout.
- No retailer affiliation, endorsement, partnership, or integration approval is
  represented.
