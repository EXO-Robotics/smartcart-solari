One-sentence description

SmartCart is a private iOS beta that turns recipes into reviewable shopping manifests, using seeded demo product records and guided retailer links without creating a retailer cart or completing checkout.

Short boilerplate

SmartCart explores a more inspectable path from recipe to grocery planning. The private validation candidate imports a recipe, lets the shopper edit and scale ingredients, applies pantry and shopping preferences, resolves seeded exact product records or labeled retailer-search fallbacks, saves the manifest, and guides the shopper to retailer destinations. Prices, availability, store details, and pickup windows are demo or prototype data—not live commerce information.
